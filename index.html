The whole picture

See all of the painting.

macOS fills the screen by cutting the painting down: tall portraits lose their heads and feet, wide panoramas lose their edges. artbip hangs the whole work and fills the space around it with a blurred echo of the painting or a colour drawn from its own palette.

Know what you're looking at

A wall label for every painting.

Each work carries a museum-style label and a link to its page at the museum. A hundred famous pictures also have a longer note on the painting, the painter and the period, and every statement in those notes is checked against a museum record or a published source.

Why this exists

Free, private and made by one person.

A habit worth keeping

Years ago I used a wallpaper app that showed a painting a day. I never studied art, but I came to know pictures I'd never have looked up. That app is gone, so I built this one for the Mac.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no analytics, no ads. Paintings download straight from the museums and Wikimedia, and the ones you already have keep rotating offline. Read the privacy policy.

Free and open source

Everything is free, under the MIT licence, with the code on GitHub. If you'd like to say thanks, you can sponsor it; that unlocks nothing.

Questions

Things people ask.

Is this legal?

Yes. Every work is public domain or CC0, and artbip records the evidence for each one (the museum's own public-domain flag, or Wikimedia's licence data) with a link to the source. Works whose US public-domain status isn't established are held back.

Does it phone home?

No. There is no artbip account, server or analytics. It downloads images directly from Wikimedia Commons and the museums' own servers, and at most once a day asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, so corrections to the collection can reach you. That check sends nothing about you, and Settings turns it off. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

It keeps rotating the paintings it has already downloaded. New images need a connection.

What does it cost in battery?

Very little. It wakes on your schedule, composes one image per display, sets it as your wallpaper and goes back to sleep.

Are there nudes?

Many great paintings include them. Works with nudity are hidden until you turn them on in Settings.

Will artbip ever cost money?

The app and everything in it today are free and will stay that way. Optional extra notes may be offered one day; nothing included now will be put behind a paywall.

Is it related to Artpip?

No. Artpip was a separate app that set fine-art wallpapers and is no longer available. artbip does a similar job and is an unaffiliated project.

I found a mistake. How do I report it?

Choose Report a Problem… in the menu while the painting is on your desktop. It opens a GitHub issue form with the work's details filled in; nothing is sent until you submit it. See support for more.
